Overview

The Salt Lick BBQ menu prices are connected to a Texas barbecue tradition that began in Driftwood in 1967. Thurman and Hisako Roberts started the restaurant after Thurman became known for his barbecue at family gatherings. He and his sons built a large barbecue pit stone by stone, and the business grew around that original pit. The Roberts family’s barbecue roots go back several generations in Texas.

At the Driftwood location, the Salt Lick BBQ menu centers on meats cooked over the family’s traditional pit. Brisket, pork ribs, sausage, turkey, pulled pork, chicken, beef ribs, and bison ribs are among the choices. The sides include beans, potato salad, and coleslaw, while homemade cobblers and pecan desserts provide a sweet finish.

The setting is a major part of the experience. The original restaurant sits among the rolling hills, oak trees, wildlife, and native wildflowers of the Texas Hill Country. The famous barbecue pit greets guests as they enter, giving the dining room a direct connection to the restaurant’s history. It makes a natural stop for families, groups, and visitors exploring the Driftwood area.

One unusual detail is that The Salt Lick was only the 14th idea on Thurman and Hisako Roberts’ list of 54 ways their family could stay in Driftwood. They first tried several other businesses before returning to barbecue. For a first order, starting with brisket and adding the traditional beans, potato salad, and coleslaw is an easy way to sample the style that built the restaurant.
