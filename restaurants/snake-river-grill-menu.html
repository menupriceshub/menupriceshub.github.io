Overview

Snake River Grill menu prices are part of a Jackson, Wyoming dining tradition that began in 1993. Founders Alan Hirschfield and August Spier created the restaurant with a focus on modern American cooking, and it has remained on Jackson’s Town Square for more than 30 years. The restaurant’s history and mountain setting have helped shape its distinctive identity.

The Snake River Grill menu changes with the seasons and brings together ingredients from local farms and ranches with carefully prepared New American dishes. Depending on the menu, diners may find prime steaks, wild game, seafood, pasta, and vegetables alongside dishes prepared in the wood-burning oven. The kitchen combines familiar ingredients with more creative combinations.

For a special meal in Jackson, Snake River Grill has a rustic-elegant dining room and an intimate bar, creating a setting suited to celebrations, date nights, or a relaxed dinner after exploring the area. Its approach is more refined than a casual mountain grill, while still keeping a connection to Jackson’s outdoor and mountain lifestyle. 1

One distinctive part of the restaurant is its seasonal approach. Rather than keeping the menu completely fixed, the kitchen updates dishes as ingredients change through the year. The restaurant also features a wood-burning oven, adding another element to its cooking style. For a first visit, choosing a seasonal main course or one of the restaurant’s prime meat dishes with a side is a natural way to explore its menu.
