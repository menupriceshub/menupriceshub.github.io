Overview

Mary Mac's Tea Room menu prices offer a look at classic Southern cooking served in the heart of Atlanta. Mary MacKenzie opened the restaurant in 1945, and it has continued to preserve the traditions and recipes that shaped its early years. Today, Mary Mac's remains the only original tea room from the group of 16 that once operated in Atlanta.

The restaurant has a warm, welcoming atmosphere designed to feel familiar and comfortable, much like a family kitchen. Its Southern cooking makes it a natural choice for family meals, celebrations, group gatherings, or anyone looking to experience traditional Atlanta dining.

The Mary Mac's Tea Room menu includes Southern favorites such as four-piece fried chicken, fried or grilled pork chops, meatloaf with tomato sauce, chicken and dumplings, and baked chicken with cornbread dressing and gravy. Guests can also choose from dishes such as fried green tomatoes, macaroni and cheese, collard greens, sweet potato soufflé, and whipped potatoes.

One distinctive part of Mary Mac's is the care given to its traditional preparation. The restaurant continues to hand-wash selected greens, snap fresh green beans, and bake its own breads and desserts, including banana pudding and Georgia peach cobbler. For an ordering tip, consider combining a traditional Southern specialty with several of the vegetable sides to experience a broader selection of the kitchen's classic flavors.
