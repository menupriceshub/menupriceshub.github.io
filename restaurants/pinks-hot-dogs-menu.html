Overview

Pink's Hot Dogs menu prices are part of a Los Angeles tradition that began in 1939, when Paul and Betty Pink started selling hot dogs from a small pushcart near La Brea and Melrose. They borrowed $50 from Betty's mother to buy the cart, and the simple business eventually grew into the family-owned Hollywood landmark that still operates from its longtime location.

The Famous Chili Dog is at the heart of the Pink's experience. The original combination pairs a hot dog with Pink's chili, mustard, and onions, while the menu has expanded into dozens of creative combinations over the years. Options include the Chili Cheese Dog, Guadalajara Dog, Chicago Polish Dog, Pastrami Reuben Dog, Mushroom Swiss Cheese Dog, and several long stretch dogs with different toppings.

Pink's is also known for its celebrity-inspired creations. The menu includes dogs named after personalities such as Martha Stewart, Rosie O'Donnell, Ozzy Osbourne, Emeril Lagasse, and Mario Lopez, along with specialty choices such as the LA Street Dog, Philly Cheesesteak Dog, Lord of the Rings Dog, and LA Chargers Dog. Burgers, chili tamales, burrito dogs, fries, and onion rings give diners alternatives to the classic hot dog.

The Hollywood location has developed its own visual identity over the decades, with signed celebrity photographs covering the dining room and a casual curbside setting dating back to the restaurant's early years. The Pink family continues to operate the business, while the original La Brea location remains the best-known expression of its Los Angeles roots. For a first visit, the Famous Chili Dog is a natural starting point for experiencing the recipe that began the Pink's story.
