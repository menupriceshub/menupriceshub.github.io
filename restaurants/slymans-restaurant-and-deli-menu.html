Slymans Restaurant And Deli Menu With Prices 2026


Overview

Slyman's Restaurant and Deli menu prices are connected to a Cleveland deli tradition that began in 1964. Located on St. Clair Avenue, the restaurant built its identity around generous deli sandwiches, especially corned beef prepared from a recipe that has remained part of the business for decades. Its straightforward approach has made it a recognizable part of Cleveland's food scene.

Corned beef is the main attraction, served deli-style on rye and available in different sandwich sizes. The menu also features roast beef, pastrami, turkey, and tuna salad, while classic grilled choices turn those meats into melts and Reubens with Swiss cheese, sauerkraut, and grilled rye. These combinations give the menu a strong old-school deli character.

There is more to explore beyond the signature sandwiches. Slyman's offers burgers, hot dogs, grilled cheese, fish sandwiches, BBQ brisket, French dip, salads, soups, potato pancakes, homemade potato salad, and coleslaw. Breakfast is also part of the menu, with choices built around eggs, corned beef, home fries, toast, and other familiar morning dishes.

A memorable part of Slyman's identity is the size and presentation of its deli sandwiches. The restaurant has long promoted its corned beef as one of its defining specialties, and its menu gives customers several ways to customize the classic combination with rye, Swiss cheese, mustard, horseradish, sauerkraut, or other additions. For a first visit, the traditional corned beef sandwich is the clearest introduction to what made the Cleveland deli famous.
