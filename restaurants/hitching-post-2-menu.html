Hitching Post 2 Menu With Prices 2026


Overview

Hitching Post 2 menu prices are connected to a Santa Ynez Valley barbecue tradition that began when the Ostini family opened the restaurant in Buellton in 1986. The family’s roots in Santa Maria-style barbecue go back to 1952, and Hitching Post 2 continues that tradition with cooking over local red oak. The restaurant is owned by Frank and Jami Ostini, with Frank closely involved in both the kitchen and winemaking.

The Hitching Post 2 menu is built around oak-grilled meats and seafood. Steaks, ribs, quail, duck, lamb, fresh fish, shrimp, and oak-grilled vegetables reflect the restaurant’s Central Coast approach. The red oak fire gives the food a smoky character while keeping the preparation closely tied to the Santa Maria barbecue tradition.

Set along Highway 246 in Buellton, Hitching Post 2 has a relaxed wine-country atmosphere that works well for a special dinner, a casual meal, or a stop while exploring the Santa Ynez Valley. The restaurant also became widely recognized after appearing in the film Sideways, with its bar becoming one of the location’s most recognizable features.

One distinctive part of Hitching Post 2 is the connection between its food and wine. Frank Ostini began making Pinot Noir specifically to pair with the restaurant’s cooking, eventually developing Hitching Post wines alongside winemaking partner Gray Hartley. For a first visit, choosing an oak-grilled steak or another signature grilled dish is a natural way to experience the restaurant’s style.
