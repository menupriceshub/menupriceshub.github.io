Overview

Minetta Tavern menu prices offer a starting point for exploring a historic New York City restaurant in Greenwich Village. Minetta Tavern originally opened in 1937 and takes its name from Minetta Brook, which once ran from 23rd Street toward the Hudson River. Over the years, the tavern became associated with writers, poets, and other notable figures.

Located on MacDougal Street, Minetta Tavern combines the character of a classic New York City tavern with the feel of a Parisian steakhouse. Its setting makes it a natural choice for a special dinner, casual gathering, or meal centered on classic French-inspired dishes and steaks.

The Minetta Tavern menu features dishes such as Steak Frites, Moules Frites, Steak Tartare, Roasted Bone Marrow, and Housemade Ravioli. Its burger selection includes the Minetta Burger with Cheddar and caramelized onions and the Black Label Burger made with prime dry-aged beef. Seafood, salads, pasta, and traditional desserts round out the menu.

One distinctive part of Minetta Tavern's history is its connection to figures including Ernest Hemingway, Ezra Pound, Eugene O'Neill, E. E. Cummings, and Dylan Thomas. For an ordering tip, consider one of the restaurant's steak or burger specialties if you want to explore the dishes most closely connected with its steakhouse-style identity.
