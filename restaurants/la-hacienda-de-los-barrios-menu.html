La Hacienda De Los Barrios Menu With Prices 2026


Overview

La Hacienda de Los Barrios menu prices are part of a San Antonio dining tradition built around home-style Mexican cooking and family recipes. The story began with Viola Barrios, who opened the original Los Barrios restaurant in 1979. La Hacienda de Los Barrios followed in 2004, continuing the family’s approach to cooking with recipes developed by Viola and a focus on welcoming guests like family.

The La Hacienda de Los Barrios menu brings together Continental Mexican and Tex-Mex favorites made with a home-cooked style. Slow-cooked beef cortadillo, vegetarian fajitas, Tex-Mex enchiladas, puffy tacos, and other traditional specialties give diners plenty of ways to explore the kitchen. The restaurant also highlights signature dishes such as El Mofofo, made with beef and pork in a special marinade before being grilled over flames.

For a meal in San Antonio, La Hacienda has a relaxed setting designed for families and groups. Its spacious outdoor patio and welcoming atmosphere make it suitable for a casual gathering, while the menu offers choices for both meat lovers and vegetarians. The restaurant’s connection to the Barrios family gives the dining experience a strong sense of tradition rather than a purely modern Tex-Mex approach.

One distinctive part of the restaurant’s story is its close connection to Viola Barrios and the recipes she perfected for the family restaurants. If you are visiting for the first time, trying one of the house specialties alongside a classic enchilada or puffy taco is a simple way to get a feel for the menu’s Mexican and Tex-Mex character.
