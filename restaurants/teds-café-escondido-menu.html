Overview

Ted Drewes Frozen Custard menu prices are part of a St. Louis dessert tradition that began in 1930, when Ted Drewes Sr. opened his first St. Louis custard stand after learning the frozen custard business while working with a carnival in Florida. The family opened the South Grand location in 1931 and the Chippewa stand in 1941, where Route 66 helped bring new customers to the business.

The signature concrete is what makes Ted Drewes especially distinctive. Created in 1959, it is a thick frozen custard treat blended with ingredients such as fruit, nuts, candy, cookies, and other toppings. It is traditionally served upside down because the custard is thick enough to stay in the cup. Popular combinations include the Cardinal Sin with cherries and hot fudge, along with many other specialty creations.

There is more to the menu than concretes. Guests can choose from classic custard cones, sundaes, malts, shakes, floats, ice cream sodas, and chocolate-dipped treats. Premium additions such as almonds, pecans, pistachios, pralines, hazelnuts, and real whipped cream allow customers to customize their dessert.

The Chippewa location has a particularly strong connection to St. Louis history. Located on historic Route 66, the stand became a familiar stop for both locals and travelers, while the South Grand location continues the family's earlier South Side tradition. Ted Drewes also has a long-running Christmas tree business, making the company part of St. Louis traditions well beyond frozen custard.
