Overview

Bomb Bomb Bar menu prices are connected to a nearly century-old South Philadelphia corner bar with a story unlike many neighborhood restaurants. Chef and owner Joey Baldino has reopened the historic Bomb Bomb, taking over from third-generation owners Frank and Deb Barbato, whose family ran the bar for 74 years. The name reportedly dates to sometime after 1936, when the neighborhood bar was bombed twice by a jealous rival.

The restaurant brings together two distinct experiences under one roof. The lively corner bar keeps the feel of a traditional South Philly neighborhood gathering place, while the back dining room continues the restaurant's Italian-American seafood tradition. A separate entrance leads into the dining room, where nautical-themed Italian artwork, red-and-white checkered cloths, original lighting, and other details preserve the room's historic character.

Italian seafood is the focus of the dining room, reflecting the restaurant's long-standing identity and Baldino's connection to Italian-American cooking. The bar, meanwhile, is designed as a casual meeting place for the neighborhood, with two TVs for sports and a convenient stop before or after events at Philadelphia's nearby stadiums.

Joey Baldino is a familiar name in South Philadelphia's restaurant scene. He is also known for reviving Palizzi Social Club, an Italian social club founded in 1918, and for Zeppoli in nearby Collingswood, New Jersey. His cooking draws on traditional Sicilian techniques and family recipes, while his work at Bomb Bomb Bar focuses on preserving the character of another historic neighborhood institution.
