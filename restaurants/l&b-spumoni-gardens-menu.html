Overview

L&B Spumoni Gardens menu prices are connected to a Brooklyn tradition that began in 1939, when Ludovico Barbati established the business on 86th Street after selling spumoni and Italian ices from a horse-drawn wagon. What began as a small frozen-dessert operation eventually grew into a full Italian-American restaurant and remains family-owned through the fourth generation.

The square Sicilian pizza is the dish most closely associated with L&B. Its thick, airy crust is baked with cheese and sauce in the restaurant's distinctive style, creating a crisp edge and soft center. The menu also includes traditional round pizzas and other Italian-American favorites, making it possible to choose between a classic slice and a full meal.

Pizza is only part of the menu. Guests can find pasta dishes, salads, heroes, seafood, meatballs, fried calamari, and other familiar Italian selections. The restaurant's catering menu also features many of its established favorites, giving the food a place in family gatherings and larger celebrations.

Of course, the name Spumoni Gardens comes from the frozen dessert that helped establish the business. Homemade spumoni and Italian ices remain important parts of the experience, providing a cool and colorful finish after a plate of pizza. For a first visit, trying the famous Sicilian square alongside a serving of spumoni gives you a taste of the two traditions that shaped the restaurant.
