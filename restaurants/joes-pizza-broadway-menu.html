Overview

Joe's Pizza menu gives you a look at the New York-style pizza served at the Broadway location in New York. Joe’s Pizza was established in 1975 by Joe Pozzuoli, who came from Naples, Italy, and built the restaurant around the classic New York street slice.

The Broadway location at 1435 Broadway has a straightforward pizza-focused style that fits a casual meal, quick bite, or stop while exploring New York. Joe’s Pizza is known for keeping its menu centered on familiar New York pizza rather than making the experience overly complicated.

The Joe's Pizza Broadway menu includes classic cheese pizza, Sicilian square pizza, fresh mozzarella pizza, white pizza, and specialty options. The Caprese Pizza combines sliced tomatoes, basil, fresh mozzarella, and extra virgin olive oil, while the Supreme Pizza includes mushrooms, onions, green peppers, sausage, and pepperoni. The menu also includes salads and beverages.

One distinctive part of Joe’s Pizza is its long connection with the New York slice tradition. The original Greenwich Village location has been serving customers since 1975, and the restaurant describes itself as a Greenwich Village institution. When ordering, a classic cheese slice is a simple way to experience the style that has defined Joe’s Pizza for decades.
