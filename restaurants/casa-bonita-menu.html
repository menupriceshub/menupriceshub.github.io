Overview

Casa Bonita menu prices are part of a Lakewood, Colorado restaurant story that began in 1974, when Bill Waugh opened the Denver-area location on West Colfax Avenue. From the beginning, the restaurant was designed as more than a place to eat, with a Mexican-village setting, elaborate scenery, live entertainment, and a large indoor waterfall. 1

The food is Mexican-inspired, but the setting is what gives a meal at Casa Bonita its unusual character. The dining experience has included dishes such as enchiladas, tacos, chicken mole, and chile relleno, while complimentary sopapillas remain one of the restaurant's best-known traditions. The current culinary program was developed under chef Dana Rodriguez after the restaurant changed ownership and underwent a major renovation. 2

There is plenty to explore beyond the dining room. Guests can encounter strolling performers, mariachi music, Black Bart's Cave, a puppet theater, and the famous cliff-diving show at the indoor waterfall. The waterfall's diving platforms and entertainment were part of what made the Lakewood location different from the other Casa Bonita restaurants that once operated elsewhere. 3

A major chapter in Casa Bonita's modern history came in 2021, when South Park creators Matt Stone and Trey Parker acquired the restaurant. After a major renovation, Casa Bonita reopened in 2023 with Rodriguez leading the kitchen. The owners kept many of the familiar features while introducing a refreshed approach to the food and dining experience. 4

If you are visiting for the first time, leaving room for the signature sopapillas is an easy way to experience one of Casa Bonita's long-running traditions. Prices may vary by location.
