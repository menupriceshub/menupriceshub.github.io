Applebee's 2 for $25: How to Maximize the Deal

Published on August 25, 2026 | Author : Akshay

Last Updated: August 2026


Applebee's 2 for $25 deal is one of the most popular value promotions in casual dining. It's simple in theory — two entrées plus an appetizer to share, all for one flat price — but a lot of guests order without knowing exactly how to stretch it. Here's an easy, no-confusion breakdown of what the deal actually includes and how to get the most food out of it.


Note: Deal pricing, included menu items, and availability can vary by location and change over time. Always confirm current details on the Applebee's app, website, or with your server before ordering.


What Is the 2 for $25 Deal?

The 2 for $25 deal lets two people each choose an entrée from a set list, plus the table gets one shareable appetizer, all bundled into a single flat price. It's built for two people dining together, whether that's a couple, friends, or a parent and a older kid splitting a meal out.


What's Usually Included

While the exact lineup can change by season and location, the deal typically covers:

  • One shareable appetizer (often chosen from a short list like boneless wings, spinach dip, or mozzarella sticks)
  • Two entrées, each selected from a set menu of Applebee's popular dishes such as burgers, chicken dishes, pasta, or steak-and-shrimp style plates

Drinks, dessert, and any premium upgrades are usually not included and will add to the bill separately.


How to Maximize the Deal

1. Check the Current Entrée List Before You Sit Down

The entrées included in the 2 for $25 lineup rotate periodically. Checking the app or website before you go means you won't be disappointed if a dish you wanted isn't part of the current promotion, and it helps you plan your order in advance.


2. Pick the Appetizer Both People Actually Want

Since the appetizer is shared, choosing something both people will eat gets you the most value. If one person doesn't eat shellfish or spice, pick a neutral option like mozzarella sticks or a shareable dip so nothing goes to waste.


3. Compare It to Ordering Separately

Before ordering, it's worth a quick mental check: would the same two entrées and appetizer cost more if ordered individually off the regular menu? In most cases, the bundle is cheaper, but confirming this helps you see exactly how much you're saving.


4. Skip Extra Drinks to Keep the Bill Low

Since drinks aren't included in the deal, ordering water instead of soda or a specialty drink keeps your final total closer to the advertised $25, especially if you're trying to stick to a set budget for the meal.


5. Ask About Add-On Upgrades

Some entrées in the deal may allow a small upgrade (like adding a topping or side) for a small extra charge. If you want a slightly bigger portion or a specific addition, ask your server what's possible within the deal before assuming you need to order a separate full-price dish.


6. Time Your Visit Around Other Promotions

Applebee's often layers additional promotions on top of the regular menu, such as discounted drinks on certain days. Pairing the 2 for $25 deal with a drink special or a loyalty reward can lower your total bill even further.


7. Use the Applebee's App for Extra Perks

Signing up for the Applebee's app or rewards program can surface additional coupons or point-based perks that stack with the 2 for $25 deal. It costs nothing to join and can turn an already good deal into an even better one over repeat visits.


Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 2 for $25 deal available every day?
Availability can vary by location and time of day, so it's best to confirm with your local Applebee's or check the app before planning your visit around it.


Can one person order both entrées to go?
Some locations allow this, but it depends on local policy. Ask your server if you'd like both portions packed to take home instead of eating in.


Does the deal include dessert?
Typically no. Dessert is usually a separate add-on and not part of the standard 2 for $25 bundle.


Final Thoughts

Applebee's 2 for $25 deal is already one of the better value options in casual dining, and a little planning makes it go even further. Check the current entrée list, pick a shared appetizer everyone will actually eat, skip pricey drinks, and see what extra perks the app offers. With those small steps, two people can walk away full without stretching the budget.
