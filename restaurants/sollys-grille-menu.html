Overview

Solly's Grille menu prices come from a Milwaukee-area restaurant tradition that began in 1936, when Kenneth “Solly” Salmon opened Solly's Coffee Shop. The family-owned restaurant later became known for the Wisconsin butter burger, and Glenn Fieber renamed it Solly's Grille in 1993 after taking over from his mother. The restaurant has been in Glendale since 1971.

The Original Solly Butter Burger is the centerpiece of the menu. Made with 100% sirloin, Wisconsin butter, and Solly's stewed onions, it has a rich, savory character that sets it apart from a typical burger. Other choices include the Double Solly Butter Burger, Cheesehead Burger, Sassy Burger, Bacon Cheeseburgers, Mushroom Swiss, Peno Burger, Bleu Cheeseburger, Patty Melt, Glendale Burger, and a veggie burger.

There is more to the menu than burgers. Solly's serves farm-style breakfast plates, breakfast sandwiches, omelets, pancakes, French toast, steaks, Wisconsin bratwurst, hot dogs, salads, sandwiches, and an Old Wisconsin fish fry featuring hand-breaded cod and shrimp. Fries, onion rings, potato pancakes, homemade soups, chili, and creamy coleslaw provide plenty of traditional sides.

The old-fashioned diner atmosphere adds another part of the Solly's experience, with counter seating and a long-running neighborhood feel. Homemade pies, malts, shakes, sundaes, floats, and other classic desserts round out the menu. For a first visit, the Original Solly Butter Burger is the most direct way to experience the restaurant's signature style, while the fish fry or farm-style breakfast offers a different side of its Wisconsin cooking tradition.
