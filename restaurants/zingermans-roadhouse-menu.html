Overview

Zingerman's Roadhouse menu prices are part of an Ann Arbor restaurant story that began in 2003, when the Roadhouse opened on Jackson Avenue with a focus on traditional American food and high-quality ingredients. The restaurant took over a building that had previously been home to Bill Knapp's, giving the location a history that reaches back well before the Roadhouse itself.

The cooking draws from American regional food traditions, with Southern influences appearing throughout the menu. Fried chicken, barbecue, macaroni and cheese, grits, biscuits, burgers, steaks, and seasonal dishes all fit into the Roadhouse's broad interpretation of American comfort food. The kitchen also explores food traditions from different parts of the country rather than limiting itself to one regional style.

Ingredient sourcing is an important part of the restaurant's approach. Zingerman's Roadhouse buys whole animals, with its in-house butcher customizing cuts and dry-aging primal cuts for as long as five weeks. The kitchen also works with local farms, American artisan cheesemakers, and other specialty producers. Its connection to Zingerman's Cornman Farms in nearby Dexter adds another local element to the menu.

A memorable chapter in the Roadhouse story began soon after opening, when pitmaster Ed Mitchell helped design and build the restaurant's pit smoker in 2004. The same year, chef Alex Young started a garden that eventually grew into a four-acre farm in Dexter, with its produce becoming an important part of the kitchen's cooking. The restaurant's macaroni and cheese also gained national attention when Alton Brown named it America's Best Comfort Food in 2010.
