Overview

Great Lakes Brewing Company menu prices are part of a Cleveland story that began in 1988, when brothers Patrick and Daniel Conway opened their brewpub in the Ohio City neighborhood. Their goal was to create an independent craft brewery for their home state, and the company has remained closely connected to Cleveland as it has grown.

The brewpub pairs its house beers with a food menu built around seasonal ingredients and Midwest comfort food. Guests can find dishes such as pretzel sticks with beer cheese, crispy pierogies, house-made hummus, chicken wings, and the Polish Boy loaded with kielbasa, coleslaw, and porter barbecue sauce. The menu also includes burgers, sandwiches, salads, fish and chips, and other larger plates.

Several dishes bring local ingredients into the dining experience. The Midwest Luxury Burger uses Ohio wagyu beef, while the classic burger features Ohio beef and Cleveland-made ingredients. Great Lakes also works with local producers for items such as sourdough, meats, cheeses, and other seasonal ingredients, giving the food a distinctly Cleveland character.

A notable part of the brewery's history is Dortmunder Gold Lager, which earned Great Lakes its first Great American Beer Festival gold medal in 1990. Christmas Ale followed as a seasonal favorite in 1992. The company also became employee-owned in 2018, adding another chapter to its long-running independent Cleveland identity.

If you are visiting the brewpub for the first time, pairing a local comfort-food dish with one of the brewery's house specialties offers a simple introduction to the experience.
