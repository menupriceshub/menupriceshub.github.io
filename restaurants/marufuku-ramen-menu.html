Marufuku Ramen Menu With Prices 2026


Overview

Marufuku Ramen menu prices are connected to a San Francisco ramen story that began with a goal of bringing authentic Hakata-style ramen to customers in the United States. The restaurant's San Francisco location is in Japan Center, where its approach centers on traditional ramen preparation and a focused menu. Marufuku describes its ramen as handmade and rooted in the Hakata style.

The Marufuku Ramen menu is built around rich, carefully prepared broths and thin artisanal noodles. Hakata Tonkotsu features a milky pork broth with chashu pork, seasoned soft-boiled egg, green onions, kikurage mushrooms, and bean sprouts. Other choices include Chicken Paitan, Shoyu Ramen, Vegetable Ramen, and Spicy TanTan Men, giving diners several different broth and topping combinations. 1

Located inside San Francisco's Japan Center, Marufuku Ramen has a modern, casual character suited to a relaxed meal with friends or a quick ramen stop. Alongside ramen, the restaurant serves small plates and rice bowls, adding variety without taking attention away from its main specialty. Its San Francisco location remains closely connected to the brand's original story. 2

One distinctive part of the cooking is the tonkotsu broth, which Marufuku says is made by boiling pork bones for long hours to develop its milky texture and umami flavor. The restaurant also uses ultra-thin artisanal noodles designed to pair with the broth. If you are ordering for the first time, Hakata Tonkotsu is a natural starting point for experiencing the style that defines Marufuku.
