Overview

Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory of Durango menu prices belong to the original store where the Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory story began in 1981. Founded by Frank Crail, the company started as a small chocolate shop on Main Avenue in Durango, Colorado. Today, the flagship store remains in downtown Durango, continuing the connection between the brand and the mountain town where it started.

Chocolate and handmade confections are the focus here, with the shop producing a wide selection of treats. Caramel apples, fudge, chocolate-covered fruits, nuts, pretzels, clusters, truffles, and other specialty chocolates give visitors plenty to explore. The store also offers packaged chocolate collections that work well for gifts, sharing, or taking a taste of Durango home.

The in-store experience is part of what makes this location notable. Guests can watch caramel apples being prepared in traditional copper kettles and see fresh fudge worked on marble slabs. The shop has also maintained the tradition of offering samples, making a visit feel more like watching a confectionery being made than simply browsing a candy store.

A fun detail from the company's early history is how its famous Mountain-Sized treats came about. Some of the first chocolates were simply too large after being completely coated in chocolate, so instead of changing their size, the company priced them by weight. That oversized approach became part of the Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory identity and remains reflected in its large chocolate bears and other treats.
