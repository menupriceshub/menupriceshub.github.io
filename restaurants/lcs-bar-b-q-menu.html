Overview

LC's Bar-B-Q menu prices are connected to an East Kansas City barbecue tradition that began in 1986, when L.C. Richardson opened the restaurant after retiring from his career at Farmland Industries. He started with a small counter attached to a liquor store and smoked the meat in a steel drum outside before the business expanded into its current space.

Barbecue is the focus here, with sliced beef, pork, ham, turkey, sausage, chicken, ribs, and burnt ends making up much of the menu. Pork burnt ends, beef burnt ends, and a half-and-half combination give diners several ways to experience the smoked-meat style associated with LC's. Sandwiches can also combine different meats with traditional barbecue sides.

The large brick pit is one of the restaurant's defining features. It was added as LC's expanded around 1990 and remains a visible part of the operation, helping create the smoky atmosphere that has long been associated with the restaurant. The location near Kansas City's Truman Sports Complex has also made LC's a familiar stop for people heading to local baseball and football games.

Traditional sides keep the menu simple, with baked beans, potato salad, coleslaw, fries, onion rings, fried mushrooms, okra, spicy green beans, and jalapeño poppers available alongside the smoked meats. For a first visit, the mixed plate is a practical way to sample ribs, two meats, and a side, while the burnt ends offer another classic LC's choice.
