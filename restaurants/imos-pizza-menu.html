Overview

Imo's Pizza menu prices are part of a St. Louis tradition that began in 1964, when Ed and Margie Imo opened their first pizza parlor at the corner of Thurman and Shaw. The couple had saved $1,500 to start the business, and their small operation eventually grew into a regional pizza brand known for its distinctive square-cut pies.

The defining feature of Imo's is its Original St. Louis Style Pizza. The crust is thin, crisp, and crunchy, with toppings spread edge to edge and the restaurant's signature Provel cheese melted across the top. Provel combines cheddar, Swiss, and provolone, giving the pizza a creamy texture that has become closely associated with St. Louis pizza.

Pizza choices range from simple cheese and build-your-own combinations to specialty selections such as the Deluxe, made with sausage, mushrooms, onions, green pepper, bacon, and Provel cheese. The menu also includes sandwiches on fresh-baked Italian bread, with options such as roast beef and cheese, turkey club, meatball and cheese, and the Imo's Extra Special.

There is plenty to choose from beyond pizza, including spaghetti, mostaccioli, baked lasagna, salads, toasted ravioli, and other appetizers. Desserts add a St. Louis touch with gooey butter cake, while brownies, Cinnimos, and New York-style cheesecake provide additional choices. For a first visit, the signature square-cut pizza with Provel cheese is the natural starting point for experiencing what made Imo's distinctive.
