Pappas Bros. Steakhouse Menu With Prices 2026


Overview

Pappas Bros. Steakhouse menu prices are connected to a Houston restaurant tradition that began in 1995 on Westheimer Road. The steakhouse opened at the former site of The Strawberry Patch, a restaurant started in 1976 by brothers Harris, Chris, and Greg Pappas. The Pappas family had already been in the restaurant business for decades, creating the foundation for a classic Texas steakhouse built around careful preparation and hospitality.

The Pappas Bros. Steakhouse menu puts prime beef at the center of the experience. Steaks are butchered in-house and selected cuts are dry-aged for at least 28 days in a humidity-controlled cooler before being seasoned with kosher salt, black pepper, and butter. Filet mignon, ribeye, New York strip, porterhouse, and bone-in cuts are joined by seafood, salads, and traditional steakhouse sides.

For a special meal in Houston, Pappas Bros. has the polished atmosphere associated with a traditional fine-dining steakhouse. It can fit occasions such as anniversaries, business meals, celebrations, or an evening when the focus is on a carefully prepared steak. The restaurant also has a major wine program supported by certified sommeliers and a large collection of selections from around the world.

One distinctive part of the kitchen is that the beef is not simply purchased and served as received. Selected cuts are butchered at the restaurant and dry-aged in-house, allowing the steakhouse to control the process from preparation through cooking. If you are ordering for the first time, choosing one of the dry-aged prime steaks with a classic side is a straightforward way to experience the restaurant's core style.
