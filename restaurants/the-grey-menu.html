Overview

The Grey menu prices offer a starting point for exploring a distinctive Southern restaurant in Savannah, Georgia. The Grey opened in December 2014 through a partnership between Johno Morisano and Chef Mashama Bailey. The restaurant occupies a carefully restored 1938 Art Deco Greyhound bus terminal, giving its dining experience a strong connection to Savannah’s history.

The restaurant combines an elegant setting with a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere. Chef Mashama Bailey brings her personal approach to Port City Southern food, using regional produce, seafood, and meats to create dishes with deep, layered flavors. The setting makes The Grey a fitting choice for a special dinner or memorable meal in Savannah.

The Grey menu focuses on Southern cooking with influences from Savannah’s coastal food traditions. Seafood, regional meats, and seasonal produce play important roles, with dishes designed around comforting flavors and carefully selected ingredients. The menu changes as the kitchen develops new ways to present its Port City Southern style.

One unique fact about The Grey is that the restaurant operates inside a former Greyhound bus terminal that was originally built in 1938. The building was painstakingly restored while preserving many elements of its original character. When ordering, consider exploring dishes featuring regional seafood or seasonal produce to experience the restaurant’s approach to Port City Southern cooking.
