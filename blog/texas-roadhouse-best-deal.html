How to Get the Best Deal at Texas Roadhouse Every Time

Published on August 25, 2026 | Author : Akshay

Last Updated: August 2026


Texas Roadhouse already has a reputation for generous portions and fair prices, but there are still simple ways to stretch your visit even further. None of these require a coupon code or a membership — just knowing what's already built into the way the restaurant runs.


Note: Hours, promotions, and pricing can vary by location and change over time. Always confirm current details with your local Texas Roadhouse or its app before planning around them.


1. Fill Up on the Free Bread and Peanuts First

Every table gets fresh-baked rolls with cinnamon butter and a bucket of roasted peanuts before the meal even starts, and both are unlimited. Pacing yourself here means you can comfortably order a smaller entrée and still leave full, which stretches your total bill further.


2. Ask About Early Dining Pricing

Many Texas Roadhouse locations offer discounted pricing on select entrées if you arrive during early dinner hours, typically before the evening rush. This isn't available everywhere or every day, so it's worth asking your server or checking your local restaurant's hours when you plan your visit.


3. Order a Full Rack Instead of Splitting a Half

This sounds backward, but on ribs and similar items, the price difference between a half rack and a full rack is often smaller than you'd expect for the amount of extra food you get. If you're hungry enough, doing the math before ordering can get you noticeably more meat per dollar.


4. Choose Sides That Add the Most Value

Every entrée already comes with two sides, so there's no reason to pay extra unless you specifically want something outside the included list. Picking heartier included sides, like a loaded baked potato or seasoned rice, adds more fullness to the plate at no additional cost.


5. Watch for Family Meal Packs

Texas Roadhouse periodically offers family-style meal packs designed to feed several people from one order, often at a lower per-person cost than ordering individual entrées. These are especially useful if you're ordering for a group or want leftovers for the next day.


6. Skip the Alcohol Markup if You're Budgeting

Like most casual dining chains, drinks (especially alcoholic ones) tend to carry a higher markup than food. If you're trying to keep the bill as close to the entrée prices as possible, sticking to water or a standard soft drink keeps costs predictable.


7. Sign Up for the VIP Club

Texas Roadhouse's free VIP Club membership typically comes with a welcome reward and occasional bonus offers sent by email. Since it costs nothing to join, it's one of the easiest ways to occasionally get free appetizers or small discounts on future visits.


8. Take Leftovers Seriously

Portions here run large, so boxing up the second half of a big steak or a large side is a simple way to turn one dinner into two meals. If you know you're not going to finish, ask for the to-go box early so you don't accidentally overeat just to avoid "wasting" food.


Frequently Asked Questions

Are the rolls and peanuts really free and unlimited?
Yes, both are provided automatically to every table at no charge and can typically be refilled on request.


Does Texas Roadhouse have a loyalty program?
Yes, the VIP Club is a free rewards program that can include a welcome offer and occasional promotions by email.


Is early dining pricing available at every location?
No, it depends on the specific restaurant and its hours, so it's best to check with your local location before relying on it.


Final Thoughts

Getting the best deal at Texas Roadhouse isn't about hunting for hidden coupons — it's about using what's already offered smartly. Pace yourself on the free bread, check for early dining pricing, pick hearty included sides, and box up leftovers when the portions run big. A few small habits like these make an already good-value restaurant go even further.
