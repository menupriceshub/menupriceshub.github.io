Does Chipotle Have a Kids Menu?

Published on August 22, 2026 | Author : Akshay

Short answer: yes, Chipotle does have a kids menu. It's not tucked away or hard to find either — it's listed right on the app and in-store menu as the "Kid's Build Your Own Meal." If you've ever wondered whether you can bring your kid to Chipotle without just ordering them a mini version of your own bowl, here's exactly how it works.


What's Actually on Chipotle's Kids Menu

Chipotle's kids meal follows the same build-your-own format as the rest of the menu, just sized down and priced for kids. A typical kids meal includes:


  • An entree — a smaller portion, and you choose the format: quesadilla, tacos, or a bowl
  • A protein — chicken, steak, barbacoa, carnitas, sofritas, or no protein at all if your kid prefers it plain
  • A side — usually chips or a piece of fruit
  • A drink — a kid-sized drink is typically included

Because it follows the same customizable format as the adult menu, you're not stuck with a fixed combination. If your kid only eats chicken and cheese, that's exactly what you can order — no substitutions needed because there's nothing rigid to substitute in the first place.


How Much Does the Chipotle Kids Meal Cost?

Chipotle's kids meal is generally priced lower than a full adult entree, and it tends to fall in a similar range to kids meals at other fast casual chains. Exact pricing varies by location and can change over time, so it's worth checking current pricing on the app or in-store rather than relying on a fixed number.


Tips for Ordering the Kids Meal

The Quesadilla Is the Most Popular Pick

For younger kids especially, the quesadilla format tends to be the easiest option — no utensils required, and it holds together well for eating in the car or at a table.


Ask About the Kids Cup for Drinks

If a drink is included, ask for the kid-sized cup specifically. It's a smaller portion than the regular drink size and is priced accordingly within the meal.


You Can Order It on the App

The kids meal is available for mobile ordering just like the rest of the menu, which can be easier than customizing it verbally at the counter, especially with a picky eater in tow.


No Age Requirement Is Typically Enforced

There isn't a strict age check for who can order the kids meal — it's generally intended for smaller appetites, so it can work for a lighter eater of any age, not just young children.


Frequently Asked Questions

Does Chipotle have a kids menu?

Yes. Chipotle offers a Kid's Build Your Own Meal option, which includes a smaller portion entree, a side, and a drink, built the same customizable way as the regular menu.


What comes with Chipotle's kids meal?

A Chipotle kids meal typically includes a smaller portion of a build-your-own entree (like a quesadilla, tacos, or a bowl), one side such as chips or fruit, and a kid-sized drink.


Can you customize a Chipotle kids meal?

Yes. Like the rest of Chipotle's menu, the kids meal is fully customizable, so you can choose the protein, format (bowl, tacos, or quesadilla), and toppings your child prefers.


Final Thoughts

Chipotle's kids menu is one of the more flexible options out there, mostly because it isn't really a separate menu at all — it's just a smaller, kid-priced version of the same build-your-own system everyone else uses. That makes it easy to get a picky eater exactly what they'll actually eat, without guessing at a fixed combo.


Prices and menu details can vary by location and change over time. For exact current pricing, check the Chipotle app or the menu at your local restaurant.
